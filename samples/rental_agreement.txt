LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

This agreement is made at Mumbai on 1 July 2026 between Mr. Rajesh Kulkarni (the Licensor) and Ms. Sneha Iyer (the Licensee) for Flat No. 304, Sai Krupa Residency, Andheri East, Mumbai.

1. The Licensor grants the Licensee permission to occupy the flat for a period of 11 months starting 1 July 2026.

2. The monthly licence fee is Rs. 28,000, payable on or before the 5th day of every month by bank transfer.

3. A late fee of Rs. 500 per day will be charged if the licence fee is not paid by the due date.

4. The Licensee has paid an interest-free security deposit of Rs. 1,00,000 which will be refunded at the end of the licence period after deducting any dues.

5. There is a lock-in period of 11 months. If the Licensee vacates before the lock-in period ends, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited by the Licensor.

6. The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time without assigning any reason by giving 7 days notice, while the Licensee must give 2 months notice.

7. The Licensor may revise the monthly licence fee at any time during the term at his sole discretion.

8. The Licensee shall use the flat only for residential purposes and shall not sub-let it to anyone.

9. The Licensor shall not be responsible for any loss, theft or damage to the belongings of the Licensee, even if caused by the negligence of the Licensor or his agents.

10. Maintenance charges of the society and electricity bills shall be paid by the Licensee.

11. Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the Licensor, and the decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding.

12. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction in all matters relating to this agreement.
